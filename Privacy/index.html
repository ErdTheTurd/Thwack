Thwack

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Summary

Thwack is an offline golf launch monitor. Camera video, ball tracking, and shot numbers are processed on your iPhone. Thwack does not create an account, does not upload your swings to our servers, and works in airplane mode.

Information Thwack uses on your device

  • Camera. Used to film your address setup and swing so Thwack can find the ball, detect impact, and measure launch. Frames are processed on-device.
  • Motion sensors. Used to check that the phone is steady and aimed correctly during setup.
  • Photos (optional). If you allow it, Thwack can save tracer videos to your Photo Library. Thwack only requests add-only access — it does not read your existing photos.
  • On-device storage. Shot history, calibration, settings, and recorded clips stay in the app’s local storage on your phone.

What we do not collect

  • No cloud account or login
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services that send your swings elsewhere
  • No sale of personal information

If Apple or another system service shows a network prompt, it is not Thwack uploading your video. Thwack’s core capture and tracking path is designed to run fully offline.

Sharing

We do not share your swing video or shot data with third parties. Anything you voluntarily share yourself (for example AirPlaying to a TV, or exporting a video from Photos) is under your control.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete shots in the app or delete the app. Uninstalling Thwack removes its local data from that device.

Children

Thwack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
