A session

Get out of the shot.

The phone does the watching. You mount it, show it the ball, and swing.

  1. Mount and aim

    Down the line: behind you, pointing at the target, about 10 feet back. Face on: beside the ball, about 8 feet away, looking at you. Any orientation on the tripod. The motion sensors tell you if the phone isn’t still and level.

  2. Lock the ball

    A white or yellow ball that stands out. Thwack finds it, locks focus and exposure, and arms for impact. Put the ball in the circle. Tap it if the lock grabbed something else. Say whether you play right- or left-handed, and which club is in your hands. Spin, later, is only a typical number for that club.

  3. Swing

    Thwack keeps the frames around impact and follows the ball while it’s still in the picture. That’s the whole measurement. The tracer is the path it saw.

  4. Read the card

    Ball speed, launch, direction, an estimated carry, an estimated spin, and the clip. Or Not sure, if the track wasn’t good enough to say.

What has to be true

iPhone
iOS 17 or later, with a slow-motion camera.
A still mount
A tripod, or the stand. If it moves, the read moves with it.
The ball
White or yellow, easy to separate from the ground and the sky.
Light
Daylight, or a bay that’s actually bright. A dark range won’t do.

Thwack

A practice tool, not a scientific launch monitor. The read depends on the light, where you put the phone, and whether the ball stands out. Use the numbers to compare shots.
