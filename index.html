Shot tracer and launch monitor

The phone watches the ball.

Set an iPhone on a tripod, address the ball, and swing. Thwack films the hit on the slow-motion camera, follows the ball through the first frames of flight, and draws the tracer. What you read next is what it could actually see.

No club sensor, no second device, no account. iOS 17 or later.

01 — The card

After the swing

A few seconds, then a card. If a figure wasn’t measured off the ball, it is marked as estimated.

  • Ball speed

    From the ball in the frames right after impact. Miles per hour or km/h.

  • Launch angle

    How steep it left, in degrees.

  • Direction

    Started left or right of the target you aimed at.

  • Carry

    Estimated. A ball-flight model turns speed, launch, and direction into yards or meters, and the card says so.

  • Spin

    Not measured. Backspin and sidespin are typical values for the club you picked. They’re labeled estimated.

  • Tracer

    The line, on the clip. Save it to Photos if you want. Thwack only asks to add photos. It doesn’t read the rest of the library.

02 — When the read is thin

Not sure.

Dim light. A ball that vanishes into the sky or the bay. A phone that got bumped. When the track isn’t good enough, Thwack says Not sure. A blank beats a ball speed it invented so the screen looked finished.

03 — Before you hit

Two places to put the phone.

Down the line, it sits behind you, pointing at the target, about 10 feet back. Face on, it sits beside the ball, about 8 feet away, looking at you. Either one. Any orientation on the mount. Tell it your handedness, and which club you’re holding. The spin estimate is just typical numbers for that club.

The sensors say if the phone isn’t still and level. Thwack finds a white or yellow ball, locks focus and exposure, and waits for the ball in the circle. Tap the ball if it locked onto the wrong thing. Then get out of the way and swing.

A tripod that doesn’t wobble is enough. Daylight, or a bay that’s actually bright. The stand Ed prints holds the phone about 15° down.

The whole sequence

04 — After the basket

It keeps the shots.

Every swing stays on the phone, numbers and clip, until you delete it. Free history is the latest ten sessions. The tracer and the launch numbers stay free either way.

Pro adds club-by-club averages — carry, ball speed, launch, and how wide you missed — plus a dispersion map, two shots side by side, and history that doesn’t stop at ten. A free week, then monthly, yearly, or a one-time unlock.

Course mode is three fictional layouts, not real courses: Pine Hollow, Cedar Ridge, and Windmill Links. You play the holes with your own launch numbers. Putting is manual.

AirPlay, or an HDMI adapter, puts ball speed, launch, and carry on a TV so you can read them from the tee. The tracer color is yours. So are the units.

Practice, in more detail

05

It does not phone home.

No account. Airplane mode is a normal way to use it. Video stays on the iPhone. Thwack doesn’t upload your swings.
