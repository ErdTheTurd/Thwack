What you get after every swing

Impact to numbers in seconds — the metrics that matter on the range.

  • Ball speed mph or km/h
  • Launch angle degrees up
  • Direction left / right of target
  • Carry estimated yards
  • Shot tracer saved to Photos

Three steps. Then hit.

No sensors on the club. No nets full of hardware. Just the phone and a ball.

  1. 01

    Mount and aim

    Tripod behind or beside the ball. Thwack checks that the phone is steady and level.

  2. 02

    Lock the ball

    Address a white or yellow ball. Thwack finds it, locks focus, and arms for impact.

  3. 03

    Swing

    High-speed capture around impact, then tracking through the first frames of flight.

On your phone.
Nowhere else.

Camera frames never leave the device for processing. No account. No cloud upload. Thwack works in airplane mode — the way a launch monitor should when you’re on the range.

What you need

If you can film a swing in daylight, you can run Thwack.

  • iPhone · iOS 17 or later, with a high-frame-rate camera
  • Tripod · steady mount — buy one or print your own
  • Ball · white or yellow, clearly visible against the background
  • Light · outdoor range or a well-lit bay — Thwack is not a night scope
