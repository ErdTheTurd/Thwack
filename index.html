What you get after every swing

Hit, wait a beat, read the numbers.

  • Ball speed mph or km/h
  • Launch angle degrees up
  • Direction left / right of target
  • Carry estimated yards
  • Shot tracer saved to Photos

Three steps. Then hit.

No club sensors. No bay full of gear. Phone and a ball.

  1. 01

    Mount and aim

    Put the phone on a stand behind or beside the ball. Thwack tells you if it’s steady and level.

  2. 02

    Lock the ball

    Use a white or yellow ball. Tap it if you need to. Thwack locks focus and waits for the hit.

  3. 03

    Swing

    It grabs the frames around impact and tracks the ball for the first bit of flight.

What you need

If you can film a swing in daylight, you’re set.

  • iPhone · iOS 17 or later, with a high-frame-rate camera
  • Tripod · a steady mount. Buy one or print your own
  • Ball · white or yellow, easy to see against the background
  • Light · outdoor range or a bright bay. Night won’t cut it
