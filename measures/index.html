What the card means

Measured, or it says so.

Hit, wait a few seconds, read it. Numbers that didn’t come straight off the ball’s flight are labeled estimated.

  • Ball speed

    Read from where the ball is in the frames just after impact. Miles per hour or km/h.

  • Launch angle

    Degrees above the ground, from that same short stretch of flight.

  • Direction

    Left or right of the target you aimed the phone at.

  • Carry

    Not a tape measure. A ball-flight model, fed by speed, launch, and direction. The card marks it estimated. Yards or meters.

  • Spin

    Not measured at all. Backspin and sidespin are typical values for the club you picked, because a phone camera doesn’t show the ball rotating. Ed hasn’t found a way around that. They’re labeled estimated.

  • Tracer

    The line drawn on the clip, from the ball Thwack actually followed. Save it to Photos, or share it. Add-only: the app doesn’t open the rest of your library.

The other result

Not sure.

If the ball blends into the background, the light goes, or the phone moves, Thwack would rather say Not sure than hand you a number it guessed.

Thwack

A practice tool, not a scientific launch monitor. The read depends on the light, where you put the phone, and whether the ball stands out. Use the numbers to compare shots.
