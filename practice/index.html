After the basket

A record, not one clip.

Every shot is saved on the phone with its numbers and its clip, until you delete it. Nothing about that leaves the device.

Free, and Pro

The tracer and the launch numbers are free. Free history keeps the latest ten sessions.

Pro is the longer memory, and the sorting. Club-by-club averages for carry, ball speed, launch, and spread. A dispersion map of where the session went, and how far you missed left or right. Two shots side by side, so you can see what changed. History that doesn’t stop at ten. A free week, then monthly, yearly, or pay once.

On a TV

AirPlay, or a wired HDMI adapter. During a session or a round, the external screen shows big ball speed, launch, and carry, so you can read them without walking back to the phone.

Tracer color is a setting. Units too — miles and yards, or metric.

Thwack

A practice tool, not a scientific launch monitor. The read depends on the light, where you put the phone, and whether the ball stands out. Use the numbers to compare shots.
